# Hi, I'm Oumaima

### Master’s Student in Artificial Intelligence | Aspiring AI Engineer

I'm currently exploring different areas of Artificial Intelligence to discover where I want to specialize.

I started with **web development**, where I enjoyed learning by creating different applications. During my Master's, I discovered **IoT** and became interested in combining software, hardware, and intelligent systems. This led me to work on projects such as **ALMA, a baby monitoring system**, and a **4WD autonomous robot designed to trace football and tennis fields**.

Currently, I'm putting most of my effort into **Generative AI and Computer Vision**, while continuing to explore other areas of AI through projects and experimentation.
